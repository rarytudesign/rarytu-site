DOM Cyprus / Real estate

Brand Identity • Web Design • Campaign Design

Project overview

DOM brings together several businesses in Cyprus, including real estate, business investment, car sales and legal services. The brief was to unite its six business areas through a consistent, recognisable visual identity.

I developed the identity and documented it in brand guidelines. The next stage was a separate advertising campaign. I designed the landing page, outdoor advertising and digital materials.
