HEVY / Influencer marketing

Brand Identity • Social Media • Art Direction

Project overview

HEVY is an influencer marketing agency connecting brands with creators. The initial brief was to create reusable social media templates, but the agency had no established visual identity.

I developed the identity and a reusable template system, produced a month’s worth of content and prepared an asset library for the internal team. The collaboration grew into ongoing art direction, design reviews and new formats for social posts and video.
