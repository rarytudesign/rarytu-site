Nomi / Payment service

Web Design • UX/UI Design

Project overview

Nomi is a payment service for freelancers working with international clients, combining invoicing, currency conversion and compliance.

I designed a marketing website to explain how the service works, including its AI exchange-rate feature, pricing and security. The focus was on helping freelancers understand what the service offers before signing up.
