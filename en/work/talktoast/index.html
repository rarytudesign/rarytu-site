Talk & Toast / Conversation club

Brand Identity • Print Design

Project overview

Talk & Toast brings people together for English conversations over breakfast.

I developed the brand identity and materials for promoting and hosting the gatherings, including posters, cards and stickers. The task was to communicate a relaxed social experience rather than a traditional language class.
